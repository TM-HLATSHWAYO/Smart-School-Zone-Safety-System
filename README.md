The Design and Implementation of a Smart School Zone Safety System focuses on improving 
safety at pedestrian crossings within school zones, where children are among the most 
vulnerable road users. This innovative system acts as a safety guardian by continuously 
monitoring crosswalk areas and detecting both pedestrians and approaching vehicles in real 
time. When activity is detected, the system automatically alerts drivers and controls traffic 
signals to ensure that pedestrians can cross safely. Importantly, the system also considers the 
fact that accidents are not only caused by drivers but can also result from children’s behaviour, 
such as inattention, distraction, or poor judgment of vehicle speed and distance. By reducing 
the need for children to make complex decisions or manually activate crossing systems, the 
technology helps compensate for these limitations. Overall, this intelligent and automated 
approach enhances safety, minimizes human error on both sides, and provides a scalable 
solution that can be implemented in school zones and other high-risk pedestrian crossing areas.
